Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 22/10/2026

Last Updated: 22/10/2026

Welcome to the LMS Platform ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you access or use our website and learning management system (the "Platform").

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described here, please discontinue use of the Platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide, maintain, and improve our educational services. The types of information we collect may include:

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account or use the Platform, we may collect:

  • Your full name and username.
  • Your email address and contact information.
  • Your password, stored in a securely hashed form where applicable.
  • Your profile picture and other profile details you choose to provide.
  • Your account role, such as student, instructor, or administrator.

1.2 Learning and Academic Information

To support learning activities, we may collect and process:

  • Course enrolments and course completion records.
  • Learning progress, lesson activity, and course history.
  • Quiz answers, assignment submissions, and assessment results.
  • Grades, feedback, certificates, and other learning achievements.
  • Messages, discussions, and other content you submit through the Platform.

1.3 Technical and Usage Information

When you access the Platform, certain information may be collected automatically, including your IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, access times, pages visited, and interactions with Platform features. This information helps us maintain the security, reliability, and performance of our services.

1.4 Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your login session, remember preferences, improve functionality, and understand how the Platform is used. Essential cookies may be necessary for the website to function correctly. Non-essential cookies, such as analytics or advertising cookies where used, will be handled according to applicable consent requirements.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the availability or functionality of some Platform features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Provide access to courses, lessons, learning materials, and assessments.
  • Track learning progress and display results or completion records.
  • Enable communication between students, instructors, and administrators.
  • Send important account, course, security, and service-related notifications.
  • Respond to support requests and resolve technical issues.
  • Monitor, prevent, and investigate fraud, misuse, and security incidents.
  • Analyse Platform usage and improve our educational services.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and enforce our policies.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not disclose personal information indiscriminately. However, information may be shared in the following circumstances:

3.1 Instructors and Administrators

Instructors and authorised administrators may access information necessary to manage courses, review submissions, provide feedback, record grades, and support learners. Access should be limited to the information required for their responsibilities.

3.2 Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to support the operation of the Platform, including hosting, database management, email delivery, analytics, security, and payment processing where applicable. These providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services, subject to appropriate contractual and security safeguards.

3.3 Legal and Security Requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect the rights and safety of users, investigate suspected wrongdoing, or protect the security of the Platform.

3.4 Business Transfers

If the Platform or its operations are involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that process, subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction. These measures may include access controls, encryption where appropriate, secure authentication practices, backups, and system monitoring.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing educational services, maintaining academic records, resolving disputes, ensuring security, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, anonymise it, or securely dispose of it, subject to any applicable retention requirements. Certain academic or administrative records may need to be retained after an account is closed.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted.
  • Object to, or request restriction of, certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on your consent.
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format, where applicable.
  • Complain to a relevant data protection authority, where applicable.

To exercise an applicable right, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Some requests may be limited by legal obligations, legitimate operational needs, or the rights of others.
