Digital Marketing: Grow Your Brand Online

This practical course teaches you how to plan, launch, and measure marketing campaigns across the channels that matter most today. You'll learn how to attract the right audience, turn attention into customers, and use data to keep improving, finishing with a portfolio of campaigns you can show to employers or clients.

What You'll Learn

  • Marketing fundamentals: Understanding audiences, positioning, and building a strategy that fits your goals
  • Search engine optimization (SEO): Keyword research, on-page optimization, and earning organic traffic
  • Paid advertising: Running and optimizing campaigns on Google Ads and social media platforms
  • Social media marketing: Building a presence, planning content calendars, and growing engagement
  • Content marketing: Writing and creating content that informs, engages, and converts
  • Email marketing: Building lists, designing campaigns, and automating customer journeys
  • Analytics and reporting: Tracking performance with Google Analytics and making data-driven decisions

How the Course Works

Each module combines short lessons with hands-on assignments, so you apply every concept right away. You'll work on real-world scenarios throughout, and the course ends with a capstone project where you build a complete marketing plan and campaign for a brand of your choice.

Who It's For

Beginners curious about marketing, small business owners who want to promote their own products, and professionals looking to expand their skills. No prior marketing experience or technical background is required.

By the End, You Will Be Able To

  • Develop a clear digital marketing strategy tied to business goals
  • Launch and manage campaigns across search, social, and email
  • Measure results and optimize based on real performance data
  • Present your work in a professional portfolio
